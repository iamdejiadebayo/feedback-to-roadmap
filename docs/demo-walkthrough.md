# Five-minute demonstration

## 1. Introduce the problem

“This is a demonstration MVP for product managers reviewing feedback across interviews, surveys, and support. It helps connect product decisions to source evidence. The target-user need is a hypothesis, and the sample data is fictional.”

## 2. Load evidence and explain the suggestion

Click **Try sample data**. Select a theme and inspect its quotes. Explain that keyword similarity suggests groups, while a person reviews their meaning. Rename a theme to describe the customer problem. Move a misplaced record or merge related groups if useful.

## 3. Show a real trade-off

Search for notification feedback. Some fictional customers ask for a daily digest, while another wants urgent updates immediately. Use the source filter and open the relevant theme. Record a decision note such as: “Investigate digest preferences with an urgent-update exception. Validate urgency and frequency before scoping.” This is a demonstrator-authored interpretation, not an AI-generated finding or validated recommendation.

## 4. Explain a priority

Enter illustrative reach of 100, impact of 2, confidence of 80%, and effort of 4 person-weeks. The score is 40. Explain that these are demonstration estimates, not measured results. Evidence volume remains separate from reach.

## 5. Deliver a usable artifact

Export the Markdown brief. Show the notes, estimates, and original feedback IDs. Download the JSON workspace and reopen it to demonstrate continuity. These two artifacts serve different needs: a readable decision document and editable working state.

## Close with boundaries and learning

“The MVP implements import, review, prioritization, and export. AI-assisted analysis is future exploration. The next product question is whether this workflow helps a real PM make and explain a decision more effectively.”

Do not claim customer adoption, time savings, model accuracy, or business impact without measuring them. Capture screenshots from the actual working app and include what you would change after testing it.
