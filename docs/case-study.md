# Case study outline

Working title: **From customer feedback to a defensible product decision**

## For Notion

1. **Context:** who this is for and the planning workflow being investigated.
2. **Problem evidence:** actual interview findings when available; currently a problem hypothesis.
3. **My role:** product framing, research planning, implementation decisions, testing, and evaluation. Explain AI-assisted development transparently where relevant.
4. **Scope:** what the first version includes and why integrations, accounts, and LLMs were deferred.
5. **Key decisions:** evidence traceability, human review, and separating frequency from reach.
6. **Demo:** screenshots or a recording of import → correction → prioritization → export.
7. **Results:** measured user/evaluation findings only; no invented impact percentages.
8. **Limitations and next steps:** failures, disagreements, and how they change the roadmap.

## Potential Substack articles

- Why a list of AI-generated themes is not a product strategy.
- Why I refused to turn feedback count into estimated reach.
- Building a baseline before adding an LLM: what the comparison should measure.
- The most useful product demo is the one you can disagree with.

These are draft angles, not published posts. Use actual build decisions and observations rather than claims of customer validation.
